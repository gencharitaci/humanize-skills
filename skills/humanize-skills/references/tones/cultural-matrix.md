# Cultural Matrix — Per-Language Tone Calibration

Read the row for the active language before applying any tone profile from
`profiles.md`. Universal rhythm targets stay universal; everything below is
what makes a tone land instead of sounding translated.

Legend: **Address** = pronoun/register system that must be right. **Trust** =
what builds credibility. **Humor** = what tolerance exists. **Taboos** =
patterns that read as AI tells or outright offenses in this language.

## English (en)

- **Address:** you, universal. First names fast; titles rare outside formal mail.
- **Trust:** concrete specifics, named sources, admitted limits. Dry wit welcome.
- **Humor:** high tolerance — irony, self-deprecation, understatement.
- **Taboos:** fake balance ("on the one hand…"), "not only… but also," sycophantic
  openers ("Great question!"), forced folksiness ("Here's the thing:").

## Chinese (zh)

- **Address:** 您 (nín) for respect, 你 for peers; titles + surname (王经理) in
  business. Dropping to 你 too fast reads rude, not warm.
- **Trust:** institutional backing, data with units, restrained tone. Modesty
  signals competence; boasting destroys it.
- **Humor:** wordplay and light self-mockery OK in informal registers; sarcasm
  toward the reader never.
- **Taboos:** exaggerated individualism ("I disrupted…"), direct "no" without
  softening in formal contexts, English-style filler calques (e.g. overuse
  of 事实上 for "in fact").

## Spanish (es)

- **Address:** tú vs. usted by region and age (Latin America: usted persists in
  formal/business; Spain: tuteo dominates). Get it wrong and the whole tone fails.
- **Trust:** warmth + competence together; cold precision reads evasive.
- **Humor:** welcome, including gentle irony; regionalisms welcome when the
  audience is known.
- **Taboos:** gerund excess ("estando siendo"), "el mismo/la misma" as pronoun,
  forced subjunctive chains, "sin embargo" stacking.

## German (de)

- **Address:** Sie default in business; du only when offered or the brand is
  explicitly informal. Never mix both in one text.
- **Trust:** facts carry weight, not adjectives. Directness is politeness.
- **Humor:** dry, sparing; none in formal/business registers.
- **Taboos:** Nominalstil piles in non-legal prose, English loanword verbs
  ("getriggert," "geboostet"), English quotation marks, "man sollte" preaching.

## French (fr)

- **Address:** vous default; tu only for peers/youth brands. "On" preferred
  over "nous" in modern professional prose.
- **Trust:** precision without rhetoric; Cartesian structure (but never announce
  the plan — no three-part plan labels).
- **Humor:** wit valued, enthusiasm distrusted; forcedangian marketing
  superlatives backfire.
- **Taboos:** "Il est important de noter" and kin, "en termes de" + noun chains,
  anglicized jargon ("scalable," "disruptif"), "n'hésitez pas à" in CTAs.

## Russian (ru)

- **Address:** вы default; ты only for intimates/youth. Name + patronymic in
  formal address.
- **Trust:** brevity, directness, concrete figures. Self-irony works informally
  and builds trust fast.
- **Humor:** high informally (including dark); zero in formal/biz.
- **Taboos:** «рады предложить,» «наша миссия,» pseudo-academic reflexive verbs,
  «осуществлять» → «делать», «данный» → «этот», long dash as AI fingerprint.

## Japanese (ja)

- **Address:** keigo system (sonkeigo/kenjougo/teineigo) by relationship; plain
  form only for intimates or declared-casual brands. A wrong level offends more
  than any word choice.
- **Trust:** humility markers, group framing over solo boasting, specifics over
  adjectives. Reserve long sentences more than English, but vary length anyway.
- **Humor:** minimal in writing; light wordplay OK in casual registers.
- **Taboos:** direct refusal without cushioning, overuse of 私 in business
  prose, English-syntax calques (excessive の chains, unnatural passive),
  emoji in anything formal.

## Turkish (tr)

- **Address:** siz default; sen for intimates/youth. Titles + Bey/Hanım in
  formal contexts.
- **Trust:** sincerity (samimiyet) + competence; flowery Ottoman-style diction
  in modern business reads as evasive AI padding.
- **Humor:** warm, conversational; proverbs sparingly and only if exact.
- **Taboos:** -mektedir piles in non-academic prose, English connective calques
  ("bununla birlikte" stacking), "bu bağlamda" throat-clearing.

## Korean (ko)

- **Address:** speech levels (합쇼체/해요체/해체) by relationship and medium;
  해요체 is the safe default for general writing. English-style "you" subjects
  (당신) in prose read as translated.
- **Trust:** diligence signals, process detail, modest framing of achievements.
- **Humor:** sparing in writing; cute/informal endings (ㅋㅋ, ~요~) only in
  declared-casual social copy.
- **Taboos:** literal translations of English transitions ("게다가" stacking),
  over-nominalization, missing topic-flow (은/는) that makes prose feel
  assembled, not written.

## Vietnamese (vi)

- **Address:** pronoun by age/status (anh/chị/em/cô/chú + tên); defaulting to
  "bạn/tôi" everywhere reads flat and foreign.
- **Trust:** diligence, study-type hedging in academic prose, concrete market
  detail in business.
- **Humor:** light, situational; avoid sarcasm toward the reader.
- **Taboos:** Sino-Vietnamese piling in plain prose, "hơn nữa" stacking, English
  marketing calques ("tối ưu hoá mọi thứ").

## Polish (pl)

- **Address:** Pan/Pani + verb always in formal contexts; ty only when offered.
  Dropping titles reads rude, not friendly.
- **Trust:** konkret — data, certificates, dates. Poles check claims before buying.
- **Humor:** irony and sarcasm work and humanize fast; none in formal registers.
- **Taboos:** English calques ("dedykowany," "serwis" for service),
  "w ramach / w zakresie" chains, -ować verb piles, "należy podkreślić" openers.

## Indonesian (id)

- **Address:** Bapak/Ibu + name formally; kamu/Anda by closeness (Anda is stiff,
  kamu is intimate — pickup is contextual, not interchangeable).
- **Trust:** warmth, community framing, religious-cultural sensitivity in tone.
- **Humor:** gentle, inclusive; avoid sarcasm and direct confrontation.
- **Taboos:** over-formal "dengan ini menyatakan" in plain prose, "selain itu"
  stacking, English buzzword salad ("leverage ekosistem sinergi").

## Ukrainian (uk)

- **Address:** Ви always formally; European business directness welcome.
  Russianisms (із-за → через, так як → бо) are the cardinal tell — political
  as well as stylistic since 2022.
- **Trust:** directness + specifics; Soviet bureaucratic residue («у зв'язку
  з,» «з метою») destroys credibility.
- **Humor:** warm, community-oriented; slightly warmer baseline than Russian.
- **Taboos:** surzhyk, «являється»/«даний» piles, Russian connective calques.

## Arabic (ar) — RTL handling still unverified, see catalog

- **Address:** MSA with appropriate honorifics (حضرتك/سيادتكم regionally);
  dialect for social copy only when the audience region is known.
- **Trust:** eloquence balanced with evidence; religious-cultural sensitivity is
  load-bearing, not decorative.
- **Humor:** situational and warm; avoid irony that can read as mockery.
- **Taboos:** dialect/MSA mixing in one text, overly literal English-transition
  calques (وعلاوة على ذلك stacking), flowery filler with no proposition.

## Portuguese (pt)

- **Address:** PT-BR conversational default ("você," "a gente"); European PT
  more formal (o senhor/a senhora). Never mix variants in one text.
- **Trust:** light self-deprecation is a human signal in BR (not
  unprofessionalism); data with reference periods in business.
- **Humor:** welcome, including internet-register informality even on LinkedIn BR.
- **Taboos:** "através de" where "com" suffices, "o mesmo/a mesma" as pronoun,
  gerund excess in EU-PT (natural in BR — do not "fix" it there).

## Italian (it)

- **Address:** Lei formally, tu for peers/youth brands; never mix. Congiuntivo
  used correctly signals education — used excessively signals assembly.
- **Trust:** precision without enthusiasm; Italians distrust superlatives, so cut
  40–60% of draft hype before styling.
- **Humor:** light irony OK; rhetorical questions ("Ha senso?") work socially.
- **Taboos:** «si passivante» piles, «nonostante/sebbene/tuttavia» stacking,
  «si rende necessario» openers, English superlatives ("best-in-class").

## Hindi (hi) — script/layout handling still unverified, see catalog

- **Address:** आप default respectful; तुम for peers, तू never in writing.
  Code-switching with English is natural in urban registers — do not sterilize it.
- **Trust:** respect markers + concrete detail; Bollywood- similes and flowery
  filler read as padding.
- **Humor:** warm, filmi references OK socially; none in formal prose.
- **Taboos:** over-Sanskritized diction in plain prose, "इसके अलावा" stacking,
  English-transition transliterations piled verbatim.

## Persian (fa) — RTL handling still unverified, see catalog

- **Address:** شما + plural verb formally; تو only intimate. Taarof
  (ritual politeness) in moderation — over-applied taarof in business prose
  reads evasive.
- **Trust:** poetic precision allowed more than in most languages, but evidence
  still required; diaspora academic prose follows international hedging norms.
- **Humor:** literary wit valued; sarcasm toward the reader never.
- **Taboos:** Arabic-register overload in plain prose, «علاوه بر این» stacking,
  English buzzwords transliterated without gloss.

## Dutch (nl) — catalog unvalidated (no source repo); treat as provisional

- **Address:** je/jij normal in most business; u for formal/elderly. Dutch
  directness is politeness — softening everything reads foreign.
- **Trust:** nuchterheid (sobriety): numbers, plain verbs, no hype.
- **Humor:** dry, understated; self-deprecation fine.
- **Taboos:** English loanword piles (" onboarden," "tackelen"), "daarnaast/
  bovendien" stacking, over-formal "hierbij" in plain prose.

## Thai (th) — catalog unvalidated (no source repo); treat as provisional

- **Address:** particles + pronouns by gender/status (ครับ/ค่ะ, ผม/ดิฉัน);
  omitting polite particles in formal prose offends more than any diction error.
- **Trust:** softeners and humility; direct confrontation avoided — bad news
  cushioned, never blunted into vagueness.
- **Humor:** playful, pun-heavy socially; gentle tone always.
- **Taboos:** blunt king-report-style directness in soft contexts, นอกจากนี้
  stacking, English buzzwords untranslated, emoji in formal prose.
